Our own collection · Japan

Sudare

Our signature Japanese bamboo, selected for grain and strength and woven with a discipline honed over generations. We craft these screens into bespoke blinds — a living surface that elevates a space through textural purity and refined simplicity.

  • Bespoke blinds
  • Hand finished

St. Gallen, Switzerland · since 1904

Jakob Schlaepfer

The St. Gallen atelier provides boundless wonder, whimsy and inspiration to couturiers, architects and fabric enthusiasts around the world; its creations have graced the catwalks of leading fashion houses. We are the Australian agents, and hold the collection — including each year's Interior capsule — for viewing in Melbourne.

  • Wallcoverings
  • Metallics
