Ian Frood
Website drafts

Three directions for the new site. They differ in structure, not just appearance — each leads with a different idea about what the business is. Have a look at all three and see which feels most like us.

All the wording is taken from our own Instagram captions — nothing has been invented. These are drafts only and are not connected to ianfrood.com.au.
  • Version A

    The Invitation

    Leads with the studio and the invitation to visit — presentations, the buying trips, seeing the collections in person. The relationship is the product. Shortest of the three.
  • Version B

    The Collections

    Leads with the cloth. A darker, gallery-like index of everything we hold — Woven Naturals, Sudare, Jakob Schlaepfer, the Frood exclusives, Buchheister hardware. The one a designer could specify from.
  • Version C

    The Workroom

    Leads with the making and the crew. Walks through how a job actually runs, from cuttings and measure through to installation. The warmest of the three.
Feedback welcome on any of it — wording, images, order of sections, or the overall direction. Mixing elements between versions is expected.
