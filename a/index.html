What you'll find

Hundreds of designs, and one or two that stop people.

The Ian Frood collection

Our own exclusive cloth — Woven Naturals in grasses, reeds and bamboo, a Japanese sudare range, grass cloths, and linens from Belgium and Italy.

Jakob Schlaepfer

The St. Gallen atelier whose creations have graced the catwalks of leading fashion houses. Fabrics, wallcoverings and the annual Interior capsule. We are the Australian agents.

Buchheister

Curtain hardware made in Germany, including the Round series. Quiet engineering for cloth that deserves it.

In the studio

We love watching people react to this collection, as they would to art.

Ian travels each year to Heimtextil, Maison & Objet and Déco Off, and to St. Gallen to see how Schlaepfer's creations are made. What comes home goes on display in Port Melbourne, where we host presentations for practices and their teams.
