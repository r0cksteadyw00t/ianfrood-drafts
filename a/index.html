Our own collection

Houses we represent

Fabrics, wallcoverings, hardware and awnings from Jakob Schlaepfer, Buchheister, G P & J Baker and Markilux — all held in the studio.

Made here

Curtains, sheers and blinds, cut, sewn and finished by our crew in the Port Melbourne workroom.

In the studio

We love watching people react to this collection, as they would to art.

Ian travels each year to Heimtextil, Maison & Objet and Déco Off, and to the mills and ateliers of the houses we represent. What comes home goes on display in Port Melbourne, where we host presentations for practices and their teams.
