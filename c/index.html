The Frood workroom · Port Melbourne

Ian Frood

Made by our crew, one small thing at a time.

Everything we sell is made here. Curtains, sheers and blinds, cut and sewn and finished by hand in Port Melbourne, for architects and designers whose projects ask for something next level.

The making

Perfectly imperfect, and that is the point.

Natural fibre gives you wobbles, knots and colour variation. Made with a craftsperson's hands rather than mass produced, it has a living quality that cannot be matched — and it is why we cut, sew and finish everything ourselves rather than sending it out.

The great joy of our work is playing a part in turning clear thinking into something visual.

How it comes together

From your drawings to the finished window.

  • CuttingsSent to studios anywhere in Australia, from our collections and the houses we represent.
  • MeasureSite measure, or we work from your drawings and schedules.
  • FabricationCut, sewn, pattern matched and hand finished in the Port Melbourne workroom.
  • InstallationInstalled to perfection by our crew, residential and commercial.
